About

Hi, I am Gerard — a Denver-based Venture Principal. My lanes are early crypto where rails meet real financial infrastructure, the boring layers that make robots and physical AI economically real, and American reindustrialization — energy, housing, manufacturing, and the bottlenecks that decide whether any of it scales.

I came up through finance, consulting, and data. That generalist path is the point: mechanisms, incentives, and bottlenecks over narratives.

What I publish: forecasts with a date, explainers with a number you can check, and the occasional contrarian take when the data disagrees with the room. If you are building settlement, compliance, robot data or actuation, energy, housing tech, manufacturing tooling, or institutional rails — DM me on X. If you are fundraising on vibes, do not.
